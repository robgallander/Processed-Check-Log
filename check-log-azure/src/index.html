Assigned checks

Source: HubSpot deals in Pending Settlement Funds (BB) and (CK). Checks Received = Checks Received Date, Email Sent = Checks Received, Type of Offer = Fees by Motion (Yes = Exclusive, No = Inclusive), Address Confirmed = Clio Address Confirmed, Assigned To = Check Coordinator. Added to List defaults to the date the deal entered its current Pending Settlement Funds stage. Added to List, Check Type, Lienholder Copy, Dispatch, Status, and Notes are kept on this page and shared with everyone who opens it. On the Processed Check Log, Month defaults to HubSpot’s Checks Reviewed Date, and processed deals stay listed after they leave Pending Settlement Funds (tagged Moved on). Each year has its own log (pick the year above the table): it includes every deal, in any stage, with a Checks Received Date in that year and Checks Under Review = Complete or Manual (No SMS). Manual rows go in the year of their Month. Rows marked Manual were added on this page for checks not tied to HubSpot; Fees, Wire, Overages, and FDO Costs rows can be marked complete with the Mark complete button, which moves them to the Processed Check Log.
